# Paradox Pastel M3 (Light Mode)

A soft, restrained, pastel sRGB Material 3 color system replacing harsh neon green with sophisticated muted tones:
- Primary: Soft Dusty Slate Blue (`#5B7C99` / `#DCE8F2`)
- Categories & Accents: Pastel Lilac/Mauve (`#82708F`), Sage Olive (`#7E8B67`), Muted Amber (`#B8864E`), Dusty Rose (`#BA6D68`), and Soft Mint/Teal (`#628E75`).
- Surface: Crisp, warm linen-white background (`#FAF9F6`) with layered soft M3 tonal surfaces (`#F6F4F0`, `#EFECE6`).
