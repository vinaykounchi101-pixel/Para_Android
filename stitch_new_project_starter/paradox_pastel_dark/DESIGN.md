## Brand & Style

This design system translates Material 3 architectural ergonomics into an atmospheric, dark obsidian realm paired with serene, powdery pastel pigments. Rejecting standard utilitarian pitch-black OLED surfaces as well as acidic, eye-straining neon accents, it establishes an elevated visual language built upon deep mineral charcoal, layered slate tones, and desaturated sRGB confectionery hues.

The design language balances tactile, physical presence with digital restraint. Deep obsidian substrates (`#0F1117`, `#161922`, `#1E2230`) provide tranquil negative space, allowing dusty slate blues, calm sage mints, buttery ambers, and gentle coral roses to function as luminous signals without inducing visual fatigue. The aesthetic marries Scandinavian color sensibility with Material Design 3 structure—precise, grounded, and quiet.

## Colors

The palette operates on calibrated contrast against an obsidian canvas. Dark surfaces follow the Material 3 tonal elevation hierarchy, beginning with base `#0F1117` and stepping upward through `#161922`, `#1E2230`, `#262B3C`, and `#2E3447`. 

Chromas are intentionally tempered:
- **Primary (`#89A8C7`)**: Dusty slate blue, delivering calm authority across primary CTAs, active segmented buttons, and focal indicators.
- **Secondary (`#BCA3CE`)**: Soft powdered lavender, used for auxiliary tags, categorical groupings, and secondary actions.
- **Tertiary / Success (`#86B29B`)**: Soft sage mint, replacing high-luminance neon green with an organic, resting positive state.
- **Warning / Near-Limit (`#E5AF74`)**: Warm pastel amber/peach providing gentle urgency without harsh alarm.
- **Error / Danger (`#E58686`)**: Muted coral rose for over-budget and destructive states.

Structural boundary lines leverage `#282D3D` for primary division and `#373E52` for interactive focus boundaries, maintaining low visual noise and maximum clarity.

## Typography

Typography relies on `Roboto Flex` for comprehensive sans-serif hierarchy, supported by `JetBrains Mono` for tabular metrics and code-adjacent figures. The variable sans provides crisp rendering across deep charcoal substrates. 

Headlines utilize weight `500` to prevent dark-mode halation or light bleed, avoiding excessively heavy display weights. Micro-copy and form labels rely on positive tracking (`0.4px` to `0.5px`) to preserve legibility when rendered in muted off-white tones against deep container backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

A strict 8pt spatial grid anchors the UI, utilizing `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for micro-alignments, chip insets, and input padding, while `space-md` (16px) through `space-xl` (32px) govern card architectures and section spacing.

The layout adapts responsively across three primary tiers:
- **Mobile (under 600px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Surfaces span full-width with 16px horizontal margins.
- **Tablet (600px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout utilizing `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Multi-card dashboards collapse into dual stacks.
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column layout maxed at 1440px canvas bounds, configured with `margin: 3rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids bright drop shadows and high-opacity drop drops. Depth is communicated primarily through Material 3 **Tonal Surface Tiers** combined with faint, crisp outlines:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#0F1117` — no elevation, no border.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: `#161922` paired with a 1px border of `#282D3D`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements / Menus)**: `#1E2230` with border `#282D3D`. Shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays)**: `#262B3C` with border `#373E52`. Shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.

Surfaces should never rely on opacity masks alone; solid container steps ensure predictable pastel contrast across all display types.

## Shapes

The design uses standard Material 3 rounded geometric curvature (`roundedness: 2`). Standard controls, text fields, and list items implement `0.5rem` (8px) corner radii. Structural surfaces like cards and content sections implement `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px). Modal sheets and oversized dialog canvases scale to `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px). Interactive status chips, pills, and standalone badges retain full circular pill rounding (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Filled Primary**: Background `#89A8C7`, text `#0F1E2C` (Roboto Flex medium, 14px), height 40px, roundedness 0.5rem. Hover scales to `#9EBEDD`.
- **Tonal (Secondary)**: Background `#352A40`, text `#F1E5F8`, border: none. Hover transitions to `#433552`.
- **Outlined**: Transparent background, 1px border in `#282D3D`, text `#89A8C7`. Hover state transitions to surface `#1E2230`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Positive / Active Chip**: Background `#1C3328`, text `#86B29B`, border 1px solid `rgba(134, 178, 155, 0.2)`. Fully rounded.
- **Warning / Near Limit**: Background `#3D2B16`, text `#E5AF74`, border 1px solid `rgba(229, 175, 116, 0.2)`.
- **Danger / Over Budget**: Background `#3D1A1A`, text `#E58686`, border 1px solid `rgba(229, 134, 134, 0.2)`.

### Input Fields
- Filled container style using `#161922` with a 1px border in `#282D3D`. Border transitions to `#89A8C7` on focus with no glowing ring. Text is `#E2E5EC`, placeholder `#9AA3B5`. Corner radius is 0.5rem.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Selected state fills with `#89A8C7` displaying dark obsidian check/dot (`#0F1E2C`). Unselected state presents a 1.5px border in `#373E52` on a transparent surface.

### Cards & Panels
- Background `#161922`, 1px border `#282D3D`, rounded-lg (16px). Padding is 1.5rem (`space-lg`). Hoverable variants shift border to `#373E52` and background to `#1E2230`.

### Data Metrics & Status Indicators
- Visual progress bars use `#1E2230` tracks with solid pastel fills: `#86B29B` (normal), `#E5AF74` (warning), `#E58686` (critical), with numbers set in `JetBrains Mono`.
